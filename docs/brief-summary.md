# Brief Summary

Leashguards is an AI-powered contract intelligence platform that helps users understand legal agreements before accepting them. It analyzes uploaded or pasted contracts, extracts important clauses, flags risky or ambiguous language, explains practical implications, and generates a plain-language risk report.

The system focuses on legal awareness rather than legal advice. It is designed for employment contracts, vendor agreements, subscriptions, rental agreements, platform terms, privacy policies, freelance agreements, and similar documents.

The prototype demonstrates:

- Clause extraction and classification.
- Severity-based risk scoring.
- Detection of one-sided, exploitative, ambiguous, privacy-invasive, financially harmful, IP-heavy, and restrictive terms.
- Practical explanations from the affected party's perspective.
- Negotiation suggestions and safer alternatives.
- A browser-based real-time dashboard.

Future versions can add OCR, DOCX/PDF parsing, LLM reasoning, RAG against legal benchmarks, vector databases, multi-agent review workflows, and jurisdiction-aware compliance checks.
