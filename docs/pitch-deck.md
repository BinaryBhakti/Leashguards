# Leashguards Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Title

Leashguards: AI Rights & Contract Intelligence System

## Slide 2: Problem

People accept employment contracts, SaaS terms, privacy policies, rental agreements, and freelance contracts without understanding hidden legal and financial risk.

## Slide 3: Why Now

Digital agreements are longer, more automated, and more one-sided. Users need legal awareness before they click accept or sign.

## Slide 4: Solution

Leashguards extracts clauses, classifies risk, explains practical consequences, compares terms against safer benchmarks, and generates negotiation recommendations.

## Slide 5: Product Demo

- Upload PDF, DOCX, text, or scanned image.
- Select agreement type, perspective, jurisdiction, project, and version.
- Run intelligence review.
- Explore findings, clauses, benchmarks, AI reasoning, scenarios, and reports.

## Slide 6: Intelligence Workflow

- Document parsing
- Clause segmentation
- Rule-based risk detection
- Benchmark comparison
- Missing safeguard detection
- LLM reasoning layer
- Report generation

## Slide 7: Risk Domains

- Employment restrictions
- Financial obligations
- Intellectual property
- Privacy and data protection
- Dispute resolution
- Liability and indemnity
- Termination and renewal
- Compliance and governance
- Ambiguity and unilateral control

## Slide 8: Differentiation

Leashguards is not a simple summarizer. It explains why clauses matter, what could happen in real life, what safeguards are missing, and what the user can negotiate.

## Slide 9: Architecture

Browser UI, Node API, document parsers, local analysis engine, optional LLM provider, local report store, PDF export, project history, and version comparison.

## Slide 10: Impact

Leashguards improves informed decision-making for individuals, freelancers, consumers, tenants, employees, and small organizations.

## Slide 11: Disclaimer

Leashguards provides legal awareness and does not replace qualified legal professionals.
